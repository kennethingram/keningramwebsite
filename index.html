About

Engineering the Future,
Leading With Purpose

I'm a technology leader with over 20 years of experience helping organisations harness emerging technology to drive genuine, lasting change. Based in the Greater Toronto Area, my work sits at the intersection of AI strategy, engineering and executive leadership.

A graduate of the University of Cambridge, I bring analytical rigour and a global perspective to every engagement — shaped by years of working alongside world-class organisations and an enduring commitment to results that matter.

My career spans consulting and leadership roles in technolgoy, consulting and engineering with specialisation in AI enablement and digital transformation. I help organisations move from curiosity about AI to confident, scaled adoption — building capability, reshaping operating models and embedding intelligence into how they work.

Outside of technology, I'm a passionate musician and perform with my band, The Covernotes. I also commit annually to charitable events throughout the GTA — believing strongly in giving back to the communities that shape us.
